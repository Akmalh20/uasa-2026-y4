Sentence 1 / Ayat 1

shì yī gòng bā líng jǐ .
是一共八零几。

Grammar Note: Incorrect word order & missing subject / Susunan perkataan tidak teratur.

Sentence 3 / Ayat 3
wǒ rán hòu xǐ liǎn , xiān chī fàn .
我然后洗脸，先吃饭。

Grammar Note: "先" (first) must come before "然后" (then) / "先" mesti didahulukan.

Question 8 / Soalan 8

Insert the correct punctuation marks / Masukkan tanda baca yang betul.

Instruction / Arahan: Study Chinese punctuation rules first / Pelajari peraturan tanda baca Cina ini dahulu.

Study Showcase: Punctuation Rules / Nota Tanda Baca

顿号 、
Enumeration Comma / Koma Senarai

Used to separate listed items or words (e.g., 面粉、糖和鸡蛋).

问号 ？
Question Mark / Tanda Soalan

Used at the end of a question (e.g., 什么节日？).

句号 。
Full Stop / Tanda Noktah

Used at the end of a complete statement (e.g., 我在洗手。).

Question 9 / Soalan 9

Unscramble words to construct correct sentences / Susun ayat dengan betul.

Instruction / Arahan: Memorize these 3 sentence structures first / Hafal 3 struktur ayat ini dahulu.

Study Showcase: Master Sentences / Hafal 3 Ayat Ini

1. Action Pattern / Ayat Action
wǒ zài yùn dòng .
我在运动。
I am exercising / Saya sedang bersenam.
2. Sequence Pattern (先 ... 然后)
bà ba xiān chī fàn , rán hòu shuā yá .
爸爸先吃饭，然后刷牙。
Dad eats first, then brushes teeth / Ayah makan dahulu, kemudian gosok gigi.
3. Time Routine Pattern / Ayat Routine Masa
bà ba zǎo shang liù shí tī zú qiú .
爸爸早上六时踢足球。
Dad plays football at 6:00 AM / Ayah bermain bola sepak pada 6:00 pagi.
